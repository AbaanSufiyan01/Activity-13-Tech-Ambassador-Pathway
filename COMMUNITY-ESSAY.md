Serving as a Campus Expert will give me the pedagogy, resources, and institutional backing to cultivate a campus culture where building in public and collaborative engineering become second nature for every REVA student.

## Part 2: Supplementary Application Responses

### Question 1: Describe a challenging technical concept you explained to a peer.
> During our data structures laboratory, several teammates were confused by the performance difference between array indexing and hash map lookups in the Two Sum problem. I drew out contiguous memory addresses on a whiteboard to illustrate how CPU cache lines benefit sequential arrays, but demonstrated how an `std::unordered_map` computes hash buckets to achieve $O(1)$ amortized lookup time, cutting our runtime from $O(N^2)$ to $O(N)$. Walking through memory diagrams made the abstract trade-off immediately tangible.

### Question 2: What resources or support do student developers at your institution need most?
> Our students need hands-on, low-stakes environments to practice modern developer workflows—specifically Git branching, semantic commit messages, automated testing, and peer code reviews. While our academic syllabus teaches algorithms and programming syntax effectively, students rarely experience realistic team pull request cycles before their final-year capstone projects.
